# E1 and E0b: first real training

Pilot. **One seed (dev world 7), 3 nights, so everything here is exploratory.** Base Qwen2.5-0.5B on 4×RTX 3090, git `b3c0c75`. All 37 cells ran; 5 hours, about $6. Tables: `results/e1_run1/analysis.txt`. Differences of a few points mean nothing here, and CIs only cover facts within this seed.

## E0b: selection is abandoned

The tuning winner, `grounding`, was tested once on seed 9: **0.776 (threshold 0.85) and 0.715 (threshold 0.75). Failed both.** A2.3's rule applies: selection is abandoned for this project, and H3 stays withdrawn. Tuning scores, by min of the two AUROCs: grounding 0.73, entropy drop 0.73 at a fifth of the cost, CHD_mix 0.58. Choosing what to teach by a signal of this kind doesn't work well enough on this model.

## What this shows

1. **Sleep gets facts into the weights.** Closed-book direct accuracy goes from 0.00 to 0.76 to 1.00 across arms, and corrected facts replace old values (`supersede` 0.93 to 1.00, `stale` 0.00). This part works.

2. **Every arm badly damages real knowledge, and I set the sweep too high to see the working range.** The best real-knowledge score is about 0.53, down from 0.997. The lowest setting tested was already overtraining: direct accuracy is 1.00 after night 0 for `B3_replay`. Higher settings are worse, and at lr 3e-4 and above training is unstable: `B3_pairs` fell to 0.14 where `B3_replay` held 1.00 at the same settings. **These numbers don't yet answer whether sleep helps; they describe an overtrained regime.**

3. **Composition doesn't happen in any arm.** Alias and two-hop are at chance after permuted-gold correction, while the open-book ceiling is 0.99 and 0.86. The E1 kill rule fired: no training arm reaches 0.5 on `acq_implied`, and the best, 0.39, comes almost entirely from reverse, which is written explicitly in the data. Pairs of real facts didn't help, despite T2's finding about co-occurrence. Neither did the teacher, though its queries are model-generated, which may be the limit.

4. **In this first read, the simple method is ahead overall.** `B4_replay_kl` has much higher acquisition than `S_core`, about the same retention, and far less drift. `S_core` has the best retention and lowest false recall, but it pays heavily in acquisition. Both beat plain replay on retention by a wide margin. This comparison is at the wrong operating point, on one seed. It's the outcome I predicted as most likely.

5. **The generic-drift alarm is blind to this damage.** `B4_replay_kl` shows drift of 0.03 nats while real knowledge falls to 0.46. KL on generic text doesn't see damage to question answering. T2 warned this metric was unvalidated. It shouldn't be used to guard knowledge, and `max_anchor_drift` built on it needs rethinking.

6. **That points at a specific cause, and a new hypothesis (H10).** Training facts in a question-and-answer frame seems to teach "in this frame, answer with taught vocabulary". Look-alike false recall climbs each night, and `taught_value_bleed` nears 1. `B4`'s anchors are generic text, and `S_core`'s are about fictional entities, so neither protects real knowledge in that frame.

7. **Damage comes mostly on night 0.** `B4` real knowledge: 0.45, 0.46, 0.46. `S_core`: 0.64, 0.57, 0.53, still sliding. `B3_replay`: 0.15, 0.11, 0.08. False recall keeps rising in all of them.

8. **The audit lead looks weak.** Known vs. fictional scored 0.96 to 0.997 in E0, but the cleaner test doesn't back it. On held-out generated queries the gap predicts correctness at 0.52 to 0.67 (threshold 0.75), and within-fact at 0.30 to 0.64. The gap also falls on never-taught control facts (-1.39 vs. -2.40 on night 0): an overtrained model gets more decisive everywhere, whether or not it knows. So my earlier suspicion of that result was right. This regime may be unfair to it, so it gets one more look at gentler settings.

## Practical

- fp16 path works on GPU: logits finite, log-probs match within 0.005 per token, a handful of skipped steps per night.
- Evaluation dominates cost: 500k tokens takes about 18 min per 3 nights for `B3`, and 2M only about 65. `S_core`'s passive audit log takes it to about 58.
